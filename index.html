INDEPENDENT RESEARCH PROJECT

Understanding public comments and unmet information needs.

Exploring how people express questions, uncertainty and information needs in public online conversations.

Project status: preliminary technical testing. A limited YouTube Data API v3 connectivity test has been completed. Large-scale cross-channel collection and analysis are on hold pending confirmation of the permitted scope from YouTube.

Research questions

Information seeking

Which information remains unclear?

Conversation context

How do questions and replies relate to the video topic?

Unmet needs

Which information could resolve unanswered questions?

Purpose and scope

Independent consumer-behavior and market research, initially focused on Korean-language public discussions of access to financial information. Findings may inform future consumer information services. Individually operated in the Republic of Korea, not affiliated with YouTube, Google or an academic institution. Not a lender, broker or credit assessment service.

The intended internal read-only client uses YouTube API Services. Proposed de-identified content analysis and aggregate observations are subject to YouTube authorization where required. No individual financial profiling, targeted advertising based on comment data, or publication of identifiable comment records is proposed.

Contact and data protection

See our privacy policy and terms of use. A private contact channel must be confirmed before review submission. Operator: GitHub profile.
